# Claim Review Standard Operating Procedure

Document type: Synthetic internal investigation guidance
Version: 1.0

## Review Sequence

The recommended review sequence is:

1. Verify claim and policy identifiers.
2. Review incident date and incident characteristics.
3. Review financial amounts.
4. Examine incident location and related claims.
5. Review available supporting evidence.
6. Consider the fraud model score.
7. Record the evidence supporting the investigation decision.

## Evidence Handling

Evidence should be distinguished into:

- direct claim attributes
- graph relationships
- retrieved procedural guidance
- machine-learning predictions

Retrieved guidance explains investigation procedures. It should not be treated as direct evidence that a particular claim is fraudulent.

## Graph Evidence

Shared attributes between claims can identify groups requiring additional review.

Investigators should examine the underlying claims rather than relying only on a graph-derived count.

## Model Evidence

A fraud probability indicates model-estimated risk under the model's training distribution.

The model score should be interpreted together with other evidence.

## Documentation

Investigation outputs should identify:

- the claim being reviewed
- relevant evidence
- graph relationships
- model signals
- retrieved guidance
- uncertainties or missing information
