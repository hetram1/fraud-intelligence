# Fraud Investigation Playbook

Document type: Synthetic internal investigation guidance
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This playbook provides structured guidance for reviewing potentially suspicious insurance claims. It is a synthetic demonstration document created for the Fraud Intelligence project.

## Initial Claim Review

An investigator should first verify the basic claim facts:

- claim identifier
- policy identifier
- incident date
- incident type
- incident location
- claimed amount
- total claim amount
- incident severity
- police report availability
- authorities contacted

The presence of one unusual attribute is not sufficient by itself to establish fraud.

## Financial Review

Investigators should compare the claimed amount with the total claim amount and review unusually large or inconsistent values.

A high claim amount should be treated as an investigation signal rather than proof of fraudulent activity.

## Relationship Review

Claims that share an incident location, incident characteristics, or other attributes may warrant additional review.

Relationship evidence should be considered together with claim-specific evidence and model-generated risk signals.

## Investigation Principle

Fraud risk should be assessed using multiple independent evidence sources.

A model prediction is a risk signal, not a final determination.

## Escalation

A case may be escalated for additional investigation when multiple independent signals are present, especially when graph relationships and claim-level evidence indicate an unusual pattern.
